## Statement

Present existing daily Personal Astrologer letters as the paid daily horoscope subscription. Offer new buyers monthly billing at USD 30 or annual billing at USD 179.82.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The paid daily horoscope subscription uses existing Personal Astrologer daily letters. Before payment, the offer identifies the covered profile, included content, and daily delivery, and distinguishes the subscription from the free horoscope for today.
- [ ] New buyers can select monthly billing at USD 30.00 or annual billing at USD 179.82. The offer and checkout show the same recurring amount and billing interval. The daily annual option shows the total annual price without the existing free-month labels.
- [ ] Existing subscriptions renew at their contracted prices.
- [ ] The subscription uses existing in-app letters and supported delivery and notification channels.
