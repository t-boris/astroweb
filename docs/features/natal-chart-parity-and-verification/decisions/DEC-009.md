## Context

The profile requests Koch, the endpoint supports all three systems, and the two dated references use Whole Sign. The contextual-help feature’s DEC-007 scoped its change to a read-only label.

## Alternatives

1. Keep Koch as the default and offer Koch, Whole Sign, and Placidus.
2. Keep the profile on Koch and use Whole Sign for verification only.
3. Default to Whole Sign and offer Koch and Placidus.

## Decision

Keep Koch as the default for the profile chart and let users select Koch, Whole Sign, or Placidus.

## Reason

Users can reproduce the dated Whole Sign examples while keeping Koch as the starting view.

## Consequences

The profile gains a house-system selector. The Houses tab continues to identify the system actually used, including a Whole Sign fallback.
