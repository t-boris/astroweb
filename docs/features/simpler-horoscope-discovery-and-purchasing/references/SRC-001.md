## Summary

The source provides qualitative stakeholder feedback about difficulties understanding service terminology and purchasing through stars. It proposes simpler names, daily, monthly and yearly horoscope offerings, and a tentative $30 daily horoscope subscription price. These are suggestions for exploration, with offer distinctions and the subscription billing period unresolved.
