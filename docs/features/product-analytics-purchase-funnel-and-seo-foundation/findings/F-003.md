## Finding

Renewal and cancel webhooks arrive as invoice and subscription events, not as checkout sessions, so checkout session metadata is not available to them. The spec also does not say whether a later withdrawal of consent affects these subsequent events.

> Checkout metadata carries the GA client_id and the consent status at checkout time.

## Possible interpretations

- Copy client_id/consent into the Stripe subscription metadata (subscription_data.metadata) and reuse them for renewals and cancels
- Always send renewals and cancels anonymously

## Resolution

При создании checkout передавать client_id, uid и consent в subscription_data.metadata. Продления и отмены читают их из metadata подписки Stripe. Отзыв согласия позже не учитывается: действует состояние на момент оформления. — see DEC-007.
