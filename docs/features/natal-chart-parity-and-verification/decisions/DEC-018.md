## Context

The arcsecond tables of the regenerated Astro-Seek charts (A-D) allow a comparison finer than the screenshots. With the Swiss Ephemeris default (true obliquity, including nutation) AstroWeb differs from Astro-Seek by up to 6.4 arcseconds on the quadrant cusps of charts C and D and by 4.2, 27.4 and 10.6 arcseconds on the Vertex of charts A, B and C. At 27 arcseconds, the Vertex of chart B would print as 5 deg 18 min Cancer where the reference screenshot (SRC-003) shows 5 deg 19 min. Computing the same ARMC with the mean obliquity (houses_armc) reproduces all 48 cusps within 1.1 arcseconds and all four Vertex values within 0.8 arcseconds. The ARMC, the planets and the nodes are unchanged.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the true obliquity for the houses. It is the Swiss Ephemeris default, but the cusps and Vertex then differ from the reference by up to 27 arcseconds and a displayed minute can differ.
2. Use the mean obliquity only for the Vertex. Cusp differences of several arcseconds against the reference would remain, and the wheel would carry two house models.

## Decision

All house-derived values (the twelve cusps, ASC, MC, DSC, IC and the Vertex) are calculated from the Swiss Ephemeris ARMC with the mean obliquity of the ecliptic. Other points are unchanged.

## Reason

It is the only model that reproduces the reference at arcsecond resolution for every house system and dataset, so the displayed degree and minute agree with the reference everywhere. Under DEC-010 a convention is adopted only when reproduction confirms it unambiguously, and here the two models differ by a factor of ten in the fit.

## Consequences

Legacy houses (cusps, asc, mc) change by at most a few arcseconds for charts calculated after this release, because the legacy houses field comes from the same calculation. Legacy aspects that have ASC or MC as an endpoint can move by 0.01 degree in the printed two-decimal orb; their pairs and types stay the same, and all aspects between bodies are unchanged. Every chart is recalculated under the new calculation version (REQ-005, I-8). The verification report records the evidence. Regression tests compare points, meta, aspect pairs and aspect types with a baseline written by origin/main before this feature.
