## Context

Reading a picture costs a vision call, and the story costs a text call. Every other paid action needs an account (DEC-001 of the storage feature).

## Alternatives

1. Free for everyone
2. Free for signed-in users with a daily limit
3. 2 ✦ (reading and story priced apart)

## Decision

Sign-in required; one star pays for the reading, the recovered birth and the story in both languages. The star is refunded when the AI fails or no birth comes out of the picture. The same picture again by the same user is free.

## Reason

Owner's choice ("Вход + 1 ✦").

## Consequences

New spend label chart_reading; TOKEN_COST.chartReading = 1 on both sides. Refunded attempts cost AI money nobody pays for, so they are capped (see the limits decision).
