## Context

DEC-003 establishes proportional redemption. The initial one-time USD prices are 4.99 for the monthly horoscope and 19.99 for the yearly horoscope.

## Alternatives

1. Set higher full-redemption thresholds, such as 6 stars for the monthly horoscope and 24 stars for the yearly horoscope.

## Decision

Set the full redemption cost at 5 stars for the current-month horoscope and 20 stars for the current-year horoscope.

## Reason

The owner selected these thresholds. At launch prices they give each redeemed star a value close to USD 1 for both horoscopes.

## Consequences

The thresholds determine the fraction of the one-time USD price covered by each redeemed star under DEC-003.
