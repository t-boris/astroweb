## Statement

The system shall read the positions printed on an uploaded natal chart picture and recover the birth date, local time, time zone and place with their accuracy, or say that no birth could be recovered and why.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The four known-answer charts read to the arcminute give their local date and time zone, the time within the reported window (about ±1 min) and the place within the reported radius (under 75 km).
- [x] A picture without ASC and MC, or without the Moon, gives 'not read' (missing_angles, missing_moon) and the star is refunded (DEC-004).
- [x] A planet that does not fit the solved moment is listed as not matched; the rest still solve.
- [x] A picture that is not a chart, a sidereal chart, or positions that belong to no moment or no place give 'not read' with the reason, and the star is refunded.
- [x] JPEG, PNG and WebP only, checked by the bytes; larger than 5 MB of base64 (about 3.9 MB of image) refused before any charge.
