## Context

F-002: the scope promises a client funnel event taxonomy (scope item 3), a Business tab in /admin backed by a new adminStats callable (scope item 6) and Google Search Console verification (scope item 7), but no requirement covers them. The owner asked most directly for the purchase funnel and for who paid, so these parts need testable acceptance criteria. Several KPI counting rules were undefined: currency handling, the timezone for day/week/month buckets, whether test-mode purchases count, and how paying users are identified when a buyer may be an anonymous deviceId or an ownerUid.

## Alternatives

1. Write requirements for the event taxonomy and Search Console now; for the Business tab record only the structure and raise currency, timezone, test purchases and the paying-user definition as separate questions to the owner (rejected: the feature would stay not implementation-ready until the owner answers)
2. Same three requirements, but convert all revenue to one reporting currency (EUR) at the purchase-date rate and bucket by the owner's timezone, e.g. Europe/Moscow (rejected: adds an exchange-rate source dependency and requires storing the rate in purchases, which changes the data schema and webhook logic)
3. Keep only the event taxonomy and Search Console in this feature and move the Business tab/adminStats (scope item 6) to a separate feature (rejected: the owner would get no Firestore-based money KPIs, only the raw purchases list and under-counting GA4 reports)

## Decision

Add three requirements: (1) the funnel event taxonomy: event names, parameters, firing points, and the rule that GA4 user_id = Firebase Auth uid only for signed-in users, with no other PII; (2) the /admin Business tab and the adminStats callable; (3) Google Search Console verification. KPI counting rules for adminStats: revenue is reported per currency separately, without conversion; day/week/month buckets are computed in UTC; test-mode purchases (livemode=false) are excluded; a paying user is identified by ownerUid, falling back to deviceId when ownerUid is absent.

## Reason

This closes the whole scope with testable acceptance criteria now, without waiting on further owner input. The default rules are simple and need no external dependencies or data-model changes: no exchange-rate source, and UTC is deterministic. Excluding livemode=false keeps revenue honest. The ownerUid→deviceId fallback counts anonymous buyers without double-counting signed-in ones.

## Consequences

New requirements for the taxonomy, Business/adminStats and Search Console are written, and F-002 can be resolved. The webhook must persist livemode in purchases (or adminStats must otherwise be able to tell test purchases apart). A buyer who paid first anonymously (deviceId) and later signed in may be counted twice unless the purchases are linked. If the owner finds UTC buckets or separate per-currency totals inconvenient, the report will need a follow-up change (e.g. owner timezone or EUR conversion).
