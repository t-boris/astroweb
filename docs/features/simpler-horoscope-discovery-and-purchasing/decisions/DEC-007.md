## Context

F-002 identified a gap in REQ-003 and REQ-005. The requirements define the initial purchase of a monthly or yearly horoscope but leave repeat access and additional purchases for the same profile and calendar period unspecified.

## Alternatives

1. Allow one purchased horoscope per profile and calendar period, with repeat access to the saved result.
2. Keep purchased horoscopes accessible after the period ends and allow users to buy additional versions for the same profile while the period is active.

## Decision

A one-time purchase grants access to one saved horoscope for the selected profile and calendar month or year. Users can reopen the saved result after the period ends. Once the purchase is made, users cannot buy another version for the same profile and calendar period.

## Reason

The team selected a single saved result per profile and period. This gives users a predictable outcome when they return and prevents duplicate purchases.

## Consequences

Update REQ-003 to require access to the saved horoscope after purchase, including after the calendar period ends. Update REQ-005 so reopening the saved result skips checkout and never charges money or deducts stars. A retry after a generation failure belongs to the original purchase and does not create a second charge.
