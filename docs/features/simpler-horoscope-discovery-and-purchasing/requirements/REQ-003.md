## Statement

Offer the existing free horoscope for today and separate personal horoscopes for the current calendar month and year. Use initial one-time prices of USD 4.99 and USD 19.99, with full redemption at 5 and 20 stars, respectively. Each purchase grants access to one saved horoscope for the selected profile and calendar period. Users can reopen it after the period ends, and each profile and calendar period allows only one purchase.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Users can find the profile-based «Гороскоп на день», «Гороскоп на месяц», and «Гороскоп на год» through their Russian period labels.
- [x] The horoscope for today reuses the existing forecast and is marked as free.
- [x] The monthly and yearly horoscopes are separate one-time purchases available without a daily subscription. Their initial base prices are USD 4.99 and USD 19.99, respectively. Full redemption requires 5 and 20 stars, respectively.
- [x] The monthly result covers the current calendar month and the yearly result covers the current calendar year, including any elapsed portion. The dates are visible before purchase and in the result.
- [x] Each monthly or yearly purchase grants access to one saved horoscope for the selected profile and calendar period.
- [x] Users can reopen the saved horoscope after purchase, including after the calendar period ends, without going through checkout, being charged money, or having stars deducted.
- [x] Once a monthly or yearly horoscope is purchased, users cannot purchase another version for the same profile and calendar period.
- [x] If generation fails after purchase, a retry uses the original purchase without charging money or deducting stars again.
