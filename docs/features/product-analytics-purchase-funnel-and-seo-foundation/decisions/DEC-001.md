## Context

Purchases and subscription events are sent from functions/src/api/stripeWebhook.ts via the GA4 Measurement Protocol, which the client-side cookie banner does not control.

## Alternatives

1. Send only when consent = granted
2. Always send with client_id

## Decision

The webhook always sends purchase and subscription start/renewal/cancel events to GA4. When the checkout metadata does not carry analytics consent = granted, the event is sent without client_id and without user_id (anonymous transaction).

## Reason

Product owner chose near-complete revenue in GA4 while not attaching identifiers for users who did not consent.

## Consequences

Anonymous purchases are not attributed to channels or funnel steps in GA4. The legal reasoning must be documented in the GA4 setup checklist. Firestore stays the financial source of truth.
