## Context

DEC-010 requires explicit point conventions but does not say how Part of Fortune picks between the day and night formula. SRC-002 (chart A) shows no Fortune, and the Astro-Seek regeneration has no setting for it. Regenerated chart B (Sun above the horizon, but in Whole Sign house 1) matches the day formula (ASC + Moon - Sun). Regenerated chart A (night) matches the night formula (ASC + Sun - Moon). A rule based on the Sun's house is therefore ruled out.

## Alternatives

1. Day chart when the Sun is in houses 7-12 (Whole Sign house of the Sun). Contradicts Astro-Seek chart B, where the Sun is in house 1 under a day formula.
2. Always the day formula (ASC + Moon - Sun). Does not match the night chart A.

## Decision

Part of Fortune is calculated with the day formula (ASC + Moon - Sun) when the Sun is above the true horizon, meaning its longitude lies between the DSC and the ASC going through the MC, and with the night formula (ASC + Sun - Moon) otherwise. The rule is independent of the house system. With an unknown birth time, Part of Fortune is not calculated.

## Reason

It reproduces both regenerated Astro-Seek charts to the arcsecond, works the same in every house system, and is the traditional definition of a day or night chart.

## Consequences

The calculator needs the ASC and the Sun longitude to pick the formula. Acceptance tests cover a day chart (B), a night chart (A) and an unknown-time chart. The verification report records the convention and the evidence.
