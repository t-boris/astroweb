## Context

The current daily subscription costs USD 19.98 per month or USD 179.82 per year. Applying the current nine-month multiplier to the new monthly price would yield USD 270 per year.

## Alternatives

1. Offer daily letters at USD 30 per month or USD 270 per year, using the existing annual pricing formula.
2. Offer daily letters at USD 30 per month with monthly billing only.

## Decision

Offer new daily horoscope subscribers a choice of USD 30 per month or USD 179.82 per year.

## Reason

The owner explicitly chose to retain the current annual price while raising the monthly price to USD 30.

## Consequences

The daily annual price is set independently of the monthly price. The offer and checkout must show the same price and billing interval.
