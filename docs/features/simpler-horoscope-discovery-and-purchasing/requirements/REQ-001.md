## Statement

Replace Oracle terminology with a plain-language situation forecast that answers the user's question and describes how the situation may develop over the next three months.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The Russian entry point, heading, actions and help consistently use a plain-language name containing «прогноз» or «гороскоп».
- [x] The description explains that the user submits a question about a situation and receives a profile-based forecast of its possible development.
- [x] The result describes how the situation may develop over the next three months from the forecast date and shows the dates covered.
