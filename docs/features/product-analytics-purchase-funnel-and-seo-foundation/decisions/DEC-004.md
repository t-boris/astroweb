## Context

Basic Consent Mode (DEC-002) needs a banner that explains what is collected; the project has no privacy policy page and no footer.

## Alternatives

1. Link to an external policy document
2. No policy link; short explanation in the banner only

## Decision

Add a short public privacy page at /privacy (English) and /ru/privacy (Russian), following the bilingual URL pattern of DEC-003. It describes GA4/Firebase Analytics, the cookies used, consent and how to change it, and the anonymised server-side purchase/subscription events sent without consent (DEC-001). The consent banner and a site footer link to it.

## Reason

No privacy/cookie page exists in web/src and no external policy was mentioned; the always-on anonymised server-side events (DEC-001) must be publicly disclosed somewhere.

## Consequences

New public routes are added to App.tsx, sitemap.xml and the hreflang set; policy text must be written (ideally legally reviewed) and kept in sync with what analytics actually collects.
