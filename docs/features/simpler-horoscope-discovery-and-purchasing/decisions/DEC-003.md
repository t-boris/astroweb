## Context

DEC-001 establishes direct service purchasing with automatic use of existing stars. The current wallet requires enough stars to cover the full cost of an action.

## Alternatives

1. Apply stars only when the balance covers the full service. Otherwise charge the full USD price and retain the balance.

## Decision

Automatically apply available stars toward a proportional share of an eligible service price and charge only the remaining amount in USD.

## Reason

Владелец выбрал доплату остатка, чтобы пользователь мог использовать даже неполный баланс.

## Consequences

Checkout must calculate and show the final USD amount before confirmation. The full star costs of the monthly and yearly horoscopes are still open.
