## Context

F-003 identifies a gap between REQ-005, which allows orders to be paid entirely with stars, and the existing purchase funnel, which uses Stripe session IDs to deduplicate purchases. Fully star-funded orders complete without a USD charge or a Stripe session.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the existing purchase funnel limited to orders with a USD payment and track full-star redemptions as a separate event and metric.

## Decision

Count each successfully completed order paid entirely with stars as a purchase with revenue of USD 0. Include orders paid in USD and orders paid entirely with stars in the same purchase conversion funnel.

## Reason

Purchase conversion should reflect all completed purchases, including those funded entirely with stars. Recording USD 0 for those orders keeps reported revenue tied to actual monetary charges.

## Consequences

Update REQ-005 and the purchase-funnel analytics requirements to record a purchase with revenue of USD 0 after a fully star-funded order completes successfully. Deduplicate these purchases by unique order ID because no Stripe session exists. Report purchase conversion across both payment paths and calculate USD revenue from the amount actually charged.
