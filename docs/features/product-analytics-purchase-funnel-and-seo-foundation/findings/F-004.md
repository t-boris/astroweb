## Finding

DEC-006 was applied only to the overview. No REQ records cover scope items 3 (client funnel event taxonomy and firing points), 6 (adminStats callable and the /admin Business tab with its counting rules) or the Search Console verification part of item 7. These are core to the owner's request (the purchase funnel and revenue KPIs). Without them they have no acceptance criteria, which is why Acceptance Criteria is marked 'partial'. Two things are also left unstated: the admin-only authorization of adminStats, and the requirement that the webhook persists livemode on purchases.
