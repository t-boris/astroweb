## Context

A refunded attempt (not a chart, no birth in it, refused by the model) still costs a vision call. A story that failed can be retried for free. Parallel uploads could pass a limit that only counts finished failures.

## Alternatives

1. No limits, like the other refunded actions
2. A general rate limiter for all callables

## Decision

Each user has at most 10 open reading attempts per UTC day: an attempt takes a slot when it starts (in the same transaction that spends the star), gives it back when it succeeds or fails through no fault of the picture (outage, missing key), and keeps it when the picture is to blame (no birth in it, or the model refused it). A lock while the same picture or the same story is in progress; an attempt older than 6 minutes (the function deadline is 5) is dead and is taken over, reusing its unrefunded star. At most 5 story attempts per reading, language and UTC day.

## Reason

Bounds the AI cost of repeated failing uploads without touching normal use. Added during implementation, beyond the approved plan.

## Consequences

Refund, failed status and the slot are written in one transaction, and only while the reading still belongs to the attempt (attempt id), so a late attempt can neither refund twice nor overwrite a newer one.
