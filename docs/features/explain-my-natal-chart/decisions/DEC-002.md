## Context

A model asked for the birth date directly guesses. The planet positions identify the moment uniquely: the slow planets the year, the Sun the day, the Moon the time to minutes; MC gives the longitude and ASC the latitude.

## Alternatives

1. Ask the model for the birth data
2. Read the birth data printed in the chart header

## Decision

The vision model returns only the printed positions as JSON (and is told not to report a name, birth date, time or place even when printed). A pure solver (astro/chartSolver.ts) finds the moment and place, checks every read point against its printed bin, and computes the time window and place radius at the edges of the bins.

## Reason

Exact, checkable and testable on known-answer charts; a misread planet is reported as not matched instead of silently shifting the answer.

## Consequences

Sidereal charts are refused (the ayanamsa is rarely printed). A picture without ASC, MC or the Moon is refused (DEC-004), so a recovered birth always has a local date, time and place. The reading is shown to the user, and the profile form opens filled in with the recovered birth, every field editable before saving.
