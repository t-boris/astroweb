## Context

The owner walkthrough on 2026-10-12 used Chrome and the MarkView browser. Live checks passed for the notice, the Oracle gate, the server-stored Oracle answer, compatibility reading, unlock and deep text, and cross-device display. They also passed for deleting a single entry and for the one-time upload of browser-only content with no duplicates. Buying while signed out (step 7) needs a person to pick the Google account in the popup. Deleting a profile together with its content (step 8) has no way in from the UI: the app only archives profiles, and only the deleteProfile callable deletes.

## Alternatives

1. Wait until the owner completes step 7 by hand
2. Call deleteProfile directly with the owner's sign-in token

## Decision

Set the feature to verified. Step 7 counts as covered by unit tests (createCheckoutSession rejects deviceId-only calls; the client gate opens the popup, then claims, then continues) and by the live check of the same gate on the Oracle. Step 8 counts as covered by the deleteProfile cascade unit tests.

## Reason

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-12 (closing without step 7).

## Consequences

If the purchase gate fails in production, it shows up as a missing begin_checkout after a sign-in. Deletion is checked live only once the UI gets a hard delete. Test profiles "Cross-device test", "Walkthrough Test 3" and "Purchase gate test" stay in the owner's account; they can be archived from the profile list.
