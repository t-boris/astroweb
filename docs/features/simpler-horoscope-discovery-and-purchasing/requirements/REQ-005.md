## Statement

Provide one checkout flow at launch for the current-month horoscope, the current-year horoscope, the situation forecast, and the relationship forecast. Automatically apply available stars in proportion to the selected service’s full star cost, show and charge the remaining USD amount, and take the user to the selected result. Set the initial terms at USD 4.99 or 5 stars for the current-month horoscope, USD 19.99 or 20 stars for the current-year horoscope, USD 1.99 or 1 star for the situation forecast, and USD 3.99 or 2 stars for the relationship forecast (DEC-010). A one-time horoscope purchase grants access to one saved result for the selected profile and calendar month or year. Users can reopen that result after the period ends without checkout, a USD charge, or a star deduction. Once purchased, the horoscope cannot be purchased again for the same profile and calendar period. If generation fails, retry it under the original purchase without another charge or star deduction. Count successfully completed orders with a USD charge and successfully completed orders paid entirely with stars in the same purchase conversion funnel. Record each fully star-funded purchase with USD 0 revenue and deduplicate it by unique order ID because no Stripe session exists. Calculate USD revenue from the amount actually charged.

- [x] The situation and relationship forecasts each use a new USD base price. Both amounts are pending owner approval.

- [ ] With no stars, the full USD base price of the selected service is payable.
