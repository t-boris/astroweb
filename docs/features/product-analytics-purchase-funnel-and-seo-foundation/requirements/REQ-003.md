## Statement

The landing page is available at / (English) and /ru (Russian), and the privacy page at /privacy (English) and /ru/privacy (Russian); each has language-specific title, description, OG/Twitter tags, canonical URL and reciprocal hreflang links, and all four URLs are listed in sitemap.xml.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] / and /ru render the landing page in English and Russian respectively, with distinct title and meta description.
- [x] /privacy and /ru/privacy render the privacy page in English and Russian respectively, with distinct title and meta description.
- [x] Each of these pages has a self-referencing canonical URL and hreflang links to its counterpart plus x-default.
- [x] sitemap.xml lists /, /ru, /privacy and /ru/privacy; it does not list /admin, /account or /profile/* routes.
- [x] Private routes (/admin, /account, /profile/*) carry noindex.
