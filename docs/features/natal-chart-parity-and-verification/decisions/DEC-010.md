## Context

REQ-002 requires lunar nodes, Black Moon Lilith, and Part of Fortune in the natal chart. The dated Astro-Seek examples show positions but do not identify the calculation conventions. Different conventions can yield different positions for the same birth data.

## Alternatives

1. Adopt fixed AstroWeb conventions: the mean North Node, the South Node 180° opposite it, mean Black Moon Lilith, and a Part of Fortune formula that reverses the order of the luminaries at night.
2. Let users choose the lunar node, Black Moon Lilith, and Part of Fortune conventions in their profile, with explicit defaults and saved preferences.

## Decision

Reproduce the dated Astro-Seek examples and adopt a calculation convention for each of the lunar nodes, Black Moon Lilith, and Part of Fortune only when the reproduction unambiguously confirms it.

## Reason

Using confirmed reference conventions gives the profile chart and its numerical acceptance tests the same basis while keeping ambiguous definitions open.

## Consequences

Record each confirmed definition in REQ-002 and use it as a matched setting in REQ-005 numerical acceptance fixtures. Retain the reproduction inputs, settings, and results as evidence. If multiple definitions match the displayed precision or the available evidence cannot distinguish them, keep that point's convention open for a separate decision.
