## Statement

The system shall offer "Explain my natal chart" next to "Create profile", where the user uploads a picture and sees when and where the person was born, the wheel, what was read, a short story about the person, and can save them as a profile under a name.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Entry points next to "Create profile" on the home page, in the profiles panel and in the profile switcher.
- [ ] Upload by choosing, dropping or pasting a picture; the page shrinks it to at most 2000 px before sending.
- [ ] The result shows the date, time ± minutes and place ± km, the match quality, the wheel, the read positions and the story in the interface language.
- [ ] Create a profile: a name plus the recovered date, time and place, all editable, saved through the existing profile creation.
- [ ] EN and RU texts with no raw keys; the page is not indexed.
