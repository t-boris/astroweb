## Context

DEC-006 fixed currency, UTC buckets, livemode and the ownerUid-to-deviceId rule, and stated that the webhook persists livemode in purchases. The code does not: purchases hold profileId, ownerDeviceId, tier, tokens, amountTotal, currency and no livemode or ownerUid. Production purchases created before this change therefore lack both fields. Subscription checkouts write no purchases document. Approved by the owner on 2026-10-12 as plan defaults.

## Alternatives

1. Backfill livemode from the Stripe API with a one-off production script
2. Exclude legacy purchases from revenue until backfilled
3. Write a purchases document for every subscription charge so revenue includes subscriptions

## Decision

1) The webhook persists livemode, ownerUid (from the wallet key when it is not a device key) and walletKey on every new purchase. 2) When a purchase has no livemode, adminStats derives it from the document id, which is the Stripe checkout session id: the prefix cs_live_ means live, cs_test_ means test; no backfill. 3) A paying user is, in order: purchase.ownerUid, the profile's ownerUid, purchase.ownerDeviceId, the profile's ownerDeviceId. 4) Revenue is the sum of purchases.amountTotal as in Acceptance Criteria #7 and does not include subscription charges; the tab shows subscription counts (active, past_due, canceled) only. 5) Period selector 7d/30d/90d/all, and a day/week/month grouping where weeks start on Monday, all in UTC. 6) ARPPU = revenue / paying users, per currency.

## Reason

Stripe session ids encode the mode, so legacy rows are classified exactly without touching production data. The payer fallback chain counts anonymous and signed-in buyers once each. Keeping revenue equal to the purchases sum is what Acceptance Criteria #7 states.

## Consequences

Subscription revenue is not visible in the Business tab or in /admin until a follow-up decision; it stays visible in Stripe and as GA4 event values. A buyer who paid anonymously and later signed in may still be counted as two payers when the profile was never claimed (DEC-006 consequence, unchanged). adminListPurchases is left as is.
