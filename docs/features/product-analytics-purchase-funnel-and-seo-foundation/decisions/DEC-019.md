## Context

Release 2.20.1 is live on natalmystic.com. At the end of the work the AI listed what was verified and what stays open: Search Console showed the submitted sitemap as 'Couldn't fetch' with an empty 'Last read', although the file is valid and Google's live URL test says it is available and can be indexed; the GA4 funnel exploration exists but has no data yet; the Business tab success path was not verified live because Google sign-in does not complete in the MarkView embedded browser (it works in Chrome, which the owner confirmed). The owner checked it and answered on 2026-10-12 that the feature can be closed.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the feature open until the sitemap status turns to Success
2. Keep it open until the first real purchase fills the funnel's last step

## Decision

The feature is closed as released. Items that depend on time or on the owner stay as owner steps in docs/ga4-setup-checklist.md section 10: re-check the sitemap status in Search Console after about 24-48 hours (re-submit it if it still fails), mark the GA4 key events, activate the Internal Traffic filter once admin events are confirmed with traffic_type = internal, optionally link BigQuery and Search Console, and look at the funnel exploration after the first real payment.

## Reason

The code, the deployment and the verification the AI could do are complete; the remaining items are controlled by Google's processing time or by the account owner.

## Consequences

REQ-007's criterion 'sitemap status Success with 4 discovered URLs' and the funnel's 'shows step-by-step drop-off' are accepted as pending, not as failed. If the sitemap stays unreadable after two days it needs a new look (finding or bug), not a reopening of this feature.
