## Statement

Buying stars and every paid action (feature unlock, paid reading generation) must require a signed-in account. An anonymous user who starts such an action is asked to sign in with Google first, their device profiles and wallet are claimed, and the action then continues.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An anonymous user who tries to buy stars or unlock a feature sees the sign-in prompt, and nothing is charged before sign-in.
- [ ] After sign-in, the user's device profiles carry ownerUid and the device wallet balance is merged into the account wallet.
- [ ] Backend purchase and unlock functions reject unauthenticated calls.
