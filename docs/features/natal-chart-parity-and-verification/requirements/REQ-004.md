## Statement

Provide the two position summaries shown beneath the supplied charts.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An element-by-modality summary shows point membership and totals for fire, earth, air and water across cardinal, fixed and mutable signs.
- [ ] A 0–30° strip shows each included point at its degree within its sign. Both summaries reconcile with the same calculated positions and their stated inclusion rules.
