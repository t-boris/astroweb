## Context

Without ASC and MC the place cannot be found, so there is no local time; the moment is known only in UTC. A chart drawn anyway (as for an unknown time, at noon) can put the Moon in another sign than the picture, and the paid story would describe it. Drawing it at the exact moment without houses needs a new mode in the shared computeNatalChart.

## Alternatives

1. Add an exact-moment and place-unknown mode to computeNatalChart
2. Give only the date and leave the Moon out of the story

## Decision

Read a picture only when it shows ASC, MC and the Moon. Otherwise the answer is "not read" with the reason (missing_angles, missing_moon, or no_place when the angles give no place), and the star is refunded. The recovered birth therefore always has a local date, time, time zone and place.

## Reason

Owner's choice (2026-11-23, after the code review found the noon-chart contradiction): keep the shared chart calculation untouched.

## Consequences

The page must say before the upload that the chart needs ASC and MC, and explain the refusal. A refused picture counts against the daily limit of DEC-003. Known limit kept: the chart is computed from the local wall-clock time, so a birth inside the repeated hour of a DST fall-back is drawn one hour off (once a year per zone).
