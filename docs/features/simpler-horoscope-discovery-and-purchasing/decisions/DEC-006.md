## Context

F-001 identified that REQ-005 did not list the services eligible for direct purchase. The current-month and current-year horoscopes already have agreed initial prices and redemption thresholds. The situation and relationship services have existing star costs and store pack prices. Their direct-purchase prices remain open.

## Alternatives

1. Include all four services and adopt the existing pack prices as direct-purchase prices: USD 0.99 for the situation forecast and USD 1.99 for the relationship forecast, with full redemption at 1 and 2 stars, respectively.
2. Limit direct purchasing in the first release to the current-month and current-year horoscopes at their agreed prices and redemption thresholds.

## Decision

Include the current-month horoscope, current-year horoscope, situation forecast, and relationship forecast in the first release of direct purchasing with automatic star redemption. Set new USD prices for the situation and relationship forecasts and retain their full-redemption thresholds of 1 and 2 stars, respectively. Keep the agreed initial terms for the current-month horoscope (USD 4.99 or 5 stars) and the current-year horoscope (USD 19.99 or 20 stars).

## Reason

The selected option extends the simplified checkout to both renamed forecasts, preserves their existing star costs, and allows the team to set USD prices for the revised services.

## Consequences

Update REQ-005 to list all four eligible services and their full-redemption thresholds. The two forecast prices still require approval before REQ-005 can specify the exact USD amounts and checkout acceptance criteria for those services. Include a test for partial redemption of the relationship forecast with one star.
