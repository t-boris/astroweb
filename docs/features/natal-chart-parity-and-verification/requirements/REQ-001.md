## Statement

Draw the profile natal wheel with every functional element visible in the three supplied examples: the chart context, the zodiac ring with a degree scale, numbered houses, the ASC, DSC, MC and IC axes, every plotted point with its position labels and retrograde marker, and the aspect lines. Let users choose Koch, Whole Sign, or Placidus with Koch as the default.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The profile chart defaults to Koch and offers Koch, Whole Sign, and Placidus.
- [ ] Selecting a system refreshes the chart for the active natal or relocation location. The wheel, positions, aspects, houses, and summaries reflect the same result.
- [ ] The chart shows the date, time, and place used. The Houses tab identifies the actual house system. If calculation falls back to Whole Sign, the requested selection and the actual system are distinguishable.
- [ ] For known birth times, the wheel shows zodiac degree marks, numbered houses, and ASC, DSC, MC, and IC at their calculated longitudes. In Whole Sign, the angles are drawn independently of the house cusps.
- [ ] For unknown birth times, the chart identifies the remaining positions as local-noon estimates and withholds angles, cusps, house assignments, and other outputs that require a known birth time.
- [ ] A wheel element inventory lists every functional element visible in SRC-002, SRC-003 and SRC-004 and maps each to the profile wheel. Logos, watermarks, fonts, colors and pixel layout are out of scope. Every other element in the inventory is drawn.
- [ ] The zodiac ring shows the twelve sign glyphs and a graduated degree scale around it.
- [ ] For known birth times, the wheel is oriented so that the house 1 cusp lies on the left (9 o'clock) axis and each house is drawn between its calculated cusps. In Whole Sign this places the ASC at its own longitude inside house 1. For unknown birth times, the zodiac is drawn from 0° Aries on the left axis.
- [ ] ASC, DSC, MC and IC are drawn as emphasized axis lines labeled with their names. Each shows its sign glyph, degree and minute at the ring end.
- [ ] Every plotted point is drawn at its calculated longitude with its glyph, degree, sign glyph and minute labels, and a retrograde marker when its motion is retrograde. A short tick marks its exact longitude on the ring. Points that lie closer together than their labels are spread apart without hiding any label.
- [ ] Aspect lines are drawn inside the inner circle between the calculated endpoints on the zodiac, including ASC and MC where the shared aspect set of REQ-003 contains an aspect to them. Major and minor aspects are distinguishable by color or line style, and line weight reflects how tight the orb is.
- [ ] Display verification compares the profile wheel for the same birth inputs against SRC-002 and SRC-003 in the MarkView browser, and against SRC-004 by element because its birth inputs are unavailable. Any inventory element that is missing is recorded.
