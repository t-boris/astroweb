## Context

The situation and relationship services are being extended to forecast how each may develop over time.

## Alternatives

1. Cover the next 30 days for both services.
2. Cover the remainder of the current calendar month for both services.

## Decision

Cover the next three months from the forecast date in both the situation forecast and the relationship forecast.

## Reason

Владелец выбрал единый трёхмесячный срок для обеих услуг.

## Consequences

Both services use the same rolling forecast window and show the dates it covers.
