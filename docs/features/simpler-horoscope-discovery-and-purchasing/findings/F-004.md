## Finding

The scope still describes access and one-time USD prices as pending, while REQ-003 specifies separate purchases without a daily subscription and gives initial prices of USD 4.99 and USD 19.99. DEC-003 also says that the star costs are still open, while DEC-004 sets them at 5 and 20 stars. Update both summaries to reflect the existing settlements so they cannot be read as release gates.

> Pending owner decisions: remaining offer structure, forecast depth, access and one-time USD prices.

## Resolution

Явно указать в Scope отдельную покупку без ежедневной подписки и стартовые цены: $4.99 за текущий месяц и $19.99 за текущий год. В DEC-003 сослаться на утверждённые пороги полного списания: 5 и 20 звёзд соответственно. — see DEC-009.
