## Finding

No requirement covers the client funnel event taxonomy (event names, parameters, firing points, user_id rules), the /admin Business tab with the adminStats callable, or Search Console verification. These are the features the owner asked for most directly (funnel, who paid). Without them there are no acceptance criteria. Undefined points include how revenue is handled across currencies, which timezone is used for day/week/month buckets, whether test-mode purchases are excluded, and how 'paying users' are counted when a buyer may be either an anonymous deviceId or an ownerUid.

> A "Business" tab in /admin (web/src/pages/Admin.tsx + a new adminStats callable in functions/src/api/admin.ts): revenue by day/week/month, purchase count, paying users, ARPPU...

## Resolution

Добавить три требования: (1) таксономия событий: имена, параметры, точки вызова и правило user_id = uid только для вошедших; (2) вкладка Business и callable adminStats; (3) верификация Search Console. Для KPI взять такие правила: выручка по валютам отдельно, без конвертации; дни, недели и месяцы считаются в UTC; покупки в тестовом режиме (livemode=false) исключаются; платящий пользователь — это ownerUid, а если его нет — deviceId. — see DEC-006.
