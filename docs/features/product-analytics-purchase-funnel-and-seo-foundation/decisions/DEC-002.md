## Context

The consent banner must gate analytics; Consent Mode v2 offers Basic (no tags before consent) and Advanced (cookieless pings with modelling).

## Alternatives

1. Advanced mode with cookieless pings and modelling

## Decision

Use Basic mode: Firebase Analytics/GA4 is not initialised and sends no requests until the user accepts analytics in the banner.

## Reason

Low initial traffic makes Advanced modelling unlikely to activate; Basic is simpler and carries the least legal risk.

## Consequences

No GA4 data for visitors who decline or ignore the banner; such visitors also have no GA client_id, so their purchases arrive in GA4 only as anonymous server-side transactions.
