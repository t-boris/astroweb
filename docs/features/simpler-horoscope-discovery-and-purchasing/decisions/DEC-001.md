## Context

USD prices already exist, but purchasing stars and consuming a service are separate steps.

## Alternatives

1. Retain separate wallet funding and service consumption.
2. Replace stars entirely with money.

## Decision

Provide one service-purchase flow with a USD amount payable and automatic application of existing stars.

## Reason

Выбран единый сценарий покупки с сохранением существующего баланса.

## Consequences

Existing balances remain usable; payment must lead to the selected service result.
