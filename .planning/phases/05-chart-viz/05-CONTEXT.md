# Phase 5: Chart Visualization - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-12
**Status:** Ready for planning

<vision>
## How This Should Work

When a user opens their profile and sees their natal chart for the first time, it should feel like a moment. The chart lives on a dark background — celestial, cosmic — like looking at a sky chart at night. Glowing lines, subtle star-like qualities. Not a static image, but something that feels alive.

The chart draws itself in when it first loads — rings appear, planets fade in, aspect lines trace their paths. It "arrives" rather than just appearing. After the reveal, the chart breathes — gentle glow pulses on planets, subtle shimmer or glow effects on the zodiac ring. It feels like looking at something real and vast.

Despite the dark magical aesthetic, everything is clearly readable. Every planet glyph, every zodiac sign, every aspect line is distinct and understandable. Beauty that obscures data is useless. The chart is an astrologer's tool wrapped in a cosmic experience.

Hovering on a planet lights up its aspect lines, dims everything else — the chart responds to exploration. Aspect filters let you strip away complexity and focus on what matters. The interactivity makes the chart feel explorable, not static.

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

- **The wow factor** — First impression of the chart must feel like a moment. Dark cosmic reveal, glowing elements, animated entrance. This is what makes the app special.
- **Readability** — Every planet, sign, house cusp, and aspect line must be clearly readable despite the dark aesthetic. Data clarity is non-negotiable.
- **Interactivity** — Hover highlighting, aspect filtering, tooltips — the chart must feel explorable and responsive. Not a static image.
- **Living feel** — After the initial reveal animation, the chart should have subtle ongoing animations that make it feel alive (gentle glow pulses, soft shimmer).

</essential>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Dark background with celestial/cosmic vibe — like looking at a night sky
- Glowing lines and elements against the dark background
- Animated reveal on load: rings draw in, planets fade/appear, aspect lines trace
- Subtle continuous animations: glow pulses on planets, shimmer on zodiac ring
- Planet hover: lights up connected aspect lines, dims everything else
- Smooth, satisfying transitions on all interactions
- No specific reference app — the general celestial/cosmic aesthetic is the guide

</specifics>

<notes>
## Additional Context

The user wants the chart to stand out from typical astrology software (which tends to look dated or purely functional). The aesthetic is the differentiator — a professional-quality chart that also feels magical and alive. All three pillars (wow factor, readability, interactivity) are equally important — no compromises.

</notes>
